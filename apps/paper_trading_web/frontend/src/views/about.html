About

A research platform for quantitative trading strategies

Develop, backtest, and paper-trade strategies in one system. Evidence from walk-forward tests and paper trading feeds a promotion process that a person must approve. Broker and live-trading paths sit behind explicit safety gates.

How it works

From idea to monitored execution

Architecture

Layers with one direction of dependency

Dependencies point downward, and no layer reaches back up. An automated check, layer_check, fails the build when code breaks this rule.

Safety

Built to fail safe

Live trading is gated

Live orders need the live_trading_enabled flag on the account. Only a human operator sets it, by hand.

A person approves promotion

A strategy moves from research to paper to live review only after a human decision. The system records each step.

Orders pass a risk gate

A pre-submit gate and kill switches check each order. A drawdown breaker stops new buys but never blocks an exit.

Evidence is out of sample

Walk-forward tests and a holdout produce the evidence. A reference note lists where backtests and live runs differ.
